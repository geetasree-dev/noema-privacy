# PRIVACY POLICY — NOEMA

**Last updated: September 2026**

## Introduction

NOEMA ("we", "our", "the app") is a personal journaling application developed by Geetasree. This Privacy Policy explains how NOEMA handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

## The Short Version

NOEMA does not collect, transmit, or share any of your personal data. Everything you write stays on your device.

## Data We Do Not Collect

NOEMA does not collect, store on any server, or share:

- Your name
- Your email address
- Your journal entries
- Your mood selections
- Your world preferences
- Your profile picture or avatar
- Your location
- Your device identifiers
- Any analytics or usage data

## Where Your Data Lives

All content you create in NOEMA — journal entries, question answers, favorite quotes, profile information, and app preferences — is stored locally on your device using Android's built-in encrypted storage system. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to any server.

We do not have servers. We do not have access to your data. We cannot read, recover, or delete anything you write.

## Data Sharing

NOEMA does not share any data with third parties. There are no analytics providers, advertising networks, or tracking services integrated into the app.

## Export Feature

NOEMA includes an optional "Export" feature that allows you to share individual entries or your full journal. When you use this feature, the text is passed to your device's native share sheet (e.g., WhatsApp, Gmail, Notes). The destination you choose is entirely under your control. NOEMA does not store, log, or transmit a copy of exported content.

## Permissions

NOEMA does not request access to:

- Your camera
- Your microphone
- Your contacts
- Your location
- Your files and storage (outside of the app's own sandbox)

## Deleting Your Data

To delete all NOEMA data, you can:

1. Open NOEMA
2. Go to Profile → Settings
3. Tap "Reset App"
4. Confirm

This permanently removes all entries, profile information, and preferences from your device.

Alternatively, uninstalling NOEMA from your device will also permanently delete all app data.

## Children's Privacy

NOEMA is not directed at children under 13. Because NOEMA does not collect any personal information, it complies with COPPA and similar regulations by design.

## Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top. Continued use of NOEMA after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
